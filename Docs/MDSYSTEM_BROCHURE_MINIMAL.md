# MDSystem Brochure

---

## What is MDSystem?

MDSystem is an enterprise-grade digital health platform providing integrated web and mobile applications for patient care, staff operations, and medical record management.

---

## Purpose

- Digitize healthcare workflows
- Empower patients with self-service access to health records and appointments
- Enhance staff efficiency with comprehensive management tools
- Centralize medical records in unified EMR system
- Enable secure real-time communication

---

## Who Uses It?

**Patients** | **Healthcare Providers** | **Administrative Staff** | **Pharmacists** | **System Administrators**
